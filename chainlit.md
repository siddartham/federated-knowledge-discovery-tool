# Dossier

Ask a question about your team's knowledge — spread across Slack, Confluence,
GitHub, Jira, and Google Drive. Dossier searches the sources, scores the
evidence, and answers with inline citations back to where each fact came from.

**Tips**
- Ask something specific — e.g. *"why did the payment-service SSO integration fail last week?"*
- Every answer carries citations you can click through to the source.
- Each run is cost-bounded and self-terminating — it stops when it's confident enough (or hits its iteration/cost cap).

See the [project README](README.md) and the [docs](docs/index.md) for how it works under the hood.
